## Whiskey AI — Provisional Workshop Schedule

### Day 1 — Context, Expertise, and Strategic Discussion
**Start:** 13:00

- **13:00–13:20** — **Welcome & Introduction**  
  Overview of the Whiskey AI initiative, workshop goals, participating communities, and expected outcomes.

- **13:20–13:50** — **Invited Talk 1**

- **13:50–14:20** — **Invited Talk 2**

- **14:20–14:50** — **Invited Talk 3**

- **14:50–15:10** — Coffee break

- **15:10–15:40** — **Invited Talk 4**

- **15:40–16:10** — **Invited Talk 5** *(optional reserve slot)*

- **16:10–17:20** — **Lightning Talks Session**  
  5-minute contributions (including questions) from participants, early-stage ideas, demos, provocations, and case studies.

- **17:20–18:20** — **Roundtable Discussion**  
  Sustainability aspects of AI-driven and process-driven fermentation, ageing, and distillation. Discussion between invited experts, organisers, and participants.

- **18:30 onward** — Informal self-funded dinner and networking *(optional)*



### Day 2 — Community Outcomes and Collaborative Writing

- **09:30–10:00** — Coffee & informal arrival

- **10:00–11:00** — **Roundtable Discussion: Call for Action Paper**  
  Identification of key themes, recommendations, opportunities, and open challenges.

- **11:00–12:15** — **Collaborative Writing Session**  
  Open drafting and breakout groups focused on sections of the paper.

- **12:15–12:45** — **Wrap-Up & Next Steps**  
  Summary of outcomes, publication strategy, future collaborations, and community coordination.

- **12:45 onward** — Informal lunch / departures

#### Notes
- Participation in the collaborative writing session is fully open and encouraged, but there is no expectation or pressure for attendees to contribute directly to the writing process.
- The schedule is intentionally flexible to accommodate discussion flow and varying levels of engagement.
- Depending on the number of submitted lightning talks, the session may be organised into thematic clusters.
