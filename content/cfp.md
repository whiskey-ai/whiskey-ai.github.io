# Call for Speakers  

**Date:** 2–3 July 2026  
**Location:** iGZW (internationales Getränkewissenschaftliches Zentrum Weihenstephan), Technical University Munich, Gregor-Mendel-Str. 4, 85354 Freising, Germany  

We invite researchers, industry practitioners, and innovators to submit abstracts for talks at the WHISKEY-AI Workshop 2026, a multidisciplinary forum exploring new approaches to **fermentation and beverage ageing processes** with a focus on improving **efficiency, quality, and sustainability**.

The workshop brings together expertise from chemical and biochemical engineering, food science, chemistry, biotechnology, instrumentation, and data-driven methods. Contributions may draw on **experimental work, mechanistic modeling, advanced sensing, or computational approaches**, including machine learning, but should emphasize relevance to real-world fermentation and production systems.

The event will run in a **noon-to-noon format** (starting at 1:00 PM on the first day and concluding at 12:00 PM on the second day) and is **free of charge** for all accepted participants.

## Format and Structure

The workshop is designed to encourage both high-level perspective and interactive exchange. The first day will focus on **plenary and keynote talks**, complemented by a **roundtable discussion** aimed at identifying key challenges, opportunities, and community priorities. The second day will adopt a more interactive, **workshop-style format**, potentially including small-group discussions and short, **flash poster presentations** (timing to be confirmed, possibly integrated into the first day).

## Topics of Interest

We welcome contributions including (but not limited to):

- Fermentation science and process optimization  
- Ageing and maturation dynamics in beverages  
- Process intensification and scale-up strategies  
- Advanced sensing, instrumentation, and process monitoring  
- Hybrid, mechanistic, and data-driven modeling approaches  
- Digital tools, including machine learning, for process understanding and control  
- Sustainable and energy-efficient production methods  
- Flavor development, quality control, and product consistency  
- Industrial case studies and technology deployment  
- Environmental impact, resource efficiency, and lifecycle analysis
